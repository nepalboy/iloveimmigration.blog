US Immigration News

Sep 14, 2026Legal migrationCNN

States sue to stop Trump rule that would make it harder for some immigrants to get green cards

Twenty-two states plus D.C. and a city coalition sued to block the expanded public-charge rule set to take effect September 18, which would let officers deny green cards over receipt of means-tested benefits — including a family member's.

Sep 14, 2026Student visasUSCIS

New I-539 and I-765 form editions required as fixed-stay student visa rule takes effect

Starting September 15, USCIS will reject older editions of the I-539 and I-765 outright as the end of "duration of status" takes effect; extension-of-stay requests now go directly to USCIS rather than through school officials.

Sep 11, 2026Legal migrationBloomberg Law

Trump administration narrows path to seek green cards inside the US

A new USCIS policy memo reframes adjustment of status as an "extraordinary" benefit, directing officers to weigh visa overstays, parole violations and other noncompliance more heavily against workers, students and family applicants already in the country.

UK professionals eligible for Canadian work permits under CPTPP — no LMIA needed
